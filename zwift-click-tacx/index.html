Which Tacx models

Tacx trainers with current firmware support Bluetooth FTMS, the standard PedalCraft uses: Neo 2T, Neo 3M, Flux S and 2, Flow Smart and others. Update the firmware in the Tacx Training app first. The very first Neo and Flux and the Vortex only speak Tacx FE-C and don't connect yet.

How shifting feels

Each gear changes the simulated grade by one percent. Shift up and the Tacx gets heavier, shift down and it gets lighter, the way it does on a road.

FAQ

Does the Tacx Neo work with the Zwift Click without Zwift?

Yes, with current firmware the Neo speaks Bluetooth FTMS and PedalCraft shifts it with the Click.

Do I need the Tacx app?

Only once to update the firmware. To ride, PedalCraft connects directly.

Does ERG mode work on Tacx?

Yes. PedalCraft Pro sends the target watts over FTMS and the Tacx holds them.
