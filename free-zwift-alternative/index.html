What you get for free

  • Smart trainer and heart rate connection via Web Bluetooth
  • Virtual shifting with Zwift Click, Play or Ride
  • Live watts, power zones, cadence, speed, grade
  • A demo mode if you want to look first

What it is not

PedalCraft has no 3D world, no group rides and no races. It is a focused tool for riding and training. If that is what you want, you save the subscription.

FAQ

Is PedalCraft really free?

The browser version is free with no time limit. Only the Mac app with workouts and the ride log is paid, once.

Which trainers work?

It is built for the Wahoo KICKR CORE 2 and works with most Bluetooth FTMS smart trainers.

Is it affiliated with Zwift?

No. PedalCraft is independent and uses Bluetooth protocols documented in the open by the indoor-cycling community.
