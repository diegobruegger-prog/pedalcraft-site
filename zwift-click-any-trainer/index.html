Which trainers work

  • Zwift Cog trainers (Wahoo KICKR CORE 2, Zwift Hub One): real virtual shifting in the trainer firmware, like in Zwift.
  • Bluetooth FTMS trainers, which is most smart trainers sold since about 2020 (for example Tacx Neo 2T and Flux S with current firmware, Elite Direto, Suito and Justo, Wahoo KICKR, Saris H3): every gear changes the simulated grade, so it rides like shifting.
  • Older trainers that only speak ANT+ or Tacx FE-C over Bluetooth don't connect yet.

Not sure? Open the free version and click Connect trainer. If your trainer shows up in the list, it works.

How to connect

  1. Quit Zwift and other trainer apps on every device. Only one app can control the trainer.
  2. Open pedalcraft.online/ride in Chrome or Edge.
  3. Click Connect trainer and pick yours.
  4. Press a button on each Click, then Connect Click.
  5. Ride. + is harder, − is easier.

Windows, Mac, Android

It runs in Chrome or Edge on Windows 10 and 11, macOS, Android and Chromebook. Your computer needs Bluetooth (built in, or a small USB Bluetooth adapter). iPhone and Safari can't do Web Bluetooth.

FAQ

Can I use the Zwift Click with a Tacx or Elite trainer?

Yes, if the trainer supports Bluetooth FTMS, which most current models do. Each Click press changes the simulated grade, so it feels like shifting gears.

Does it work on Windows?

Yes, in Chrome or Edge on Windows 10 or 11 with Bluetooth.
