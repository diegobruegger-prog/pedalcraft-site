Monthly or annual?

The annual plan ($199.99) only pays off if you keep Zwift for more than 10 months a year. Most people ride indoors from about October to March, so cancelling the monthly plan ($19.99) every summer is usually cheaper: six months cost about $120 a year.

US list prices, checked October 2026. Prices differ by country and change; check Zwift before you subscribe.

What you lose without Zwift

The virtual worlds, group rides, races and Zwift's training plans. If you mostly ride alone, shift with your Zwift Click and look at your watts, you don't need them.

FAQ

How much does Zwift cost per year?

$199.99 on the annual plan, or $19.99 a month. Six indoor months on the monthly plan cost about $120.

Is the Zwift annual plan worth it?

Only if you ride Zwift more than about 10 months a year. Otherwise monthly and cancelling in summer is cheaper.

Can I keep using my Zwift Click after cancelling?

Yes. PedalCraft connects the Click and your trainer in the browser without a Zwift subscription.
