Which Elite models

Elite smart trainers with Bluetooth FTMS work, which covers the current Direto, Suito, Justo and Avanti models. Update the firmware in Elite's myETraining app if the trainer doesn't show up.

How to connect

  1. Quit Zwift on every device.
  2. Open pedalcraft.online/ride in Chrome or Edge.
  3. Connect trainer, pick your Elite, then connect the Click.

What you get

Live watts, cadence, speed and zones, shifting with the Click, and grade changes with the arrow keys. PedalCraft Pro adds ERG workouts, an FTP test and a ride log.

FAQ

Can the Zwift Click shift an Elite Suito?

Yes. PedalCraft turns each Click press into a grade change on the Suito over Bluetooth FTMS.

Does it need Zwift?

No. It runs in your browser without the Zwift app or an account.

Windows or Mac?

Both, plus Android and Chromebook, in Chrome or Edge.
