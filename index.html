Questions.

Do I need a Zwift account?

No. PedalCraft talks to your trainer and shifters directly over Bluetooth. Quit Zwift on all devices before riding.

Which trainers work?

It is built for the Wahoo KICKR CORE 2. Trainers with Zwift virtual shifting get firmware shifting, other Bluetooth FTMS trainers get grade-based gears and ERG workouts.

Windows, iPhone, Safari?

The browser version works in Chrome and Edge on Windows, Mac and Android. iPhone and Safari don't support Web Bluetooth. The full app is Mac only for now.

My Click v2 “−” stops after a minute.

Zwift locks the left puck unless you rode in Zwift recently. PedalCraft restarts it every 50 seconds, so “easier” may pause for a second now and then. The right puck is never locked.

How do I install the Mac app?

After buying you get a download link by email and in your Gumroad library. Unzip PedalCraft.zip and double-click Install.command. The first time, macOS asks once: click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Allow Bluetooth, done.

What about my data?

No account, no tracking. The browser version keeps nothing. The Mac app stores rides only on your Mac. We count anonymous page visits and connections, nothing else.
