After buying you get a download link by email and in your Gumroad library.

  1. Unzip PedalCraft.zip and double-click Install.command.
  2. The first time, macOS asks once: click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Allow Bluetooth. Done. Updates are announced inside the app.

FAQ

Do I need a Zwift account?

No. PedalCraft talks to your trainer and shifters directly over Bluetooth. Please quit Zwift on all devices before riding.

Does it work on Windows or iPhone?

The free browser version works in Chrome and Edge on Windows, Mac and Android. iPhone and Safari don't support Web Bluetooth. The full app is Mac only for now.

My Click v2 «−» stops working after a minute

Zwift locks the left puck unless you rode in Zwift recently. PedalCraft restarts it every 50 seconds, so «easier» may pause for a second now and then. The right puck is never locked.

Which trainers work?

It is built for the KICKR CORE 2. Trainers with Zwift virtual shifting get firmware shifting, other Bluetooth FTMS trainers get grade-based gears and ERG workouts.

What about my data?

No account, no tracking. The browser version keeps nothing. The Mac app stores rides only on your Mac. We count anonymous page visits and connections, nothing else.
