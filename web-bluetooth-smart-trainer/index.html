Which browsers work

  • Chrome and Edge on macOS, Windows and Android: yes
  • Safari, Firefox and all browsers on iPhone: no, they don't support Web Bluetooth

What the browser talks to

  • FTMS, the standard Bluetooth fitness machine profile: power, cadence, speed and resistance
  • Zwift's virtual shifting protocol on Zwift Cog trainers, so the trainer firmware shifts
  • Zwift Click, Play and Ride controllers for the shift buttons
  • The standard heart rate profile

Privacy

Everything happens in your browser tab. PedalCraft has no account and no server for your data; the free version keeps nothing once you close the tab.

FAQ

Why does Chrome ask me to pick each device?

Web Bluetooth only connects to devices you choose yourself, one at a time. That is a privacy feature of the browser.

Does it work offline?

The page needs to load once; after that the Bluetooth connection is local between your browser and the trainer.
