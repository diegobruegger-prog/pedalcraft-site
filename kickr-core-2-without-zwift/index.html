What still works without Zwift

  • Virtual shifting with the Zwift Click, done by the trainer firmware
  • Live power, cadence, speed and your power zones
  • Grade changes with the arrow keys
  • Heart rate from a strap or a watch that broadcasts heart rate

Start in two minutes

  1. Quit Zwift on all devices.
  2. Open pedalcraft.online/ride in Chrome or Edge.
  3. Connect the trainer, then the Click.
  4. Pedal and shift.

FAQ

Can I use the KICKR CORE 2 virtual shifting without Zwift?

Yes. PedalCraft sends the gear to the trainer the same way Zwift does, so the trainer firmware shifts.

Do I need the Wahoo app?

No. PedalCraft connects to the trainer directly over Bluetooth.

Will my rides be saved?

Not in the free browser version. The Mac app saves every ride and can upload it to Strava.
