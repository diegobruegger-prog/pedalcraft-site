The left Zwift Click stops after a minute

You connect your Zwift Click v2 outside the Zwift app, both buttons work, and after about a minute the left «−» puck goes quiet. It's not broken. Here's what's going on.

Start ridingTry the demo ›

Why it happens

The Click v2 left puck is locked by its firmware unless the Zwift app unlocked it recently. Outside Zwift it answers for about a minute after connecting, then stops sending button presses. The right «+» puck is never locked.

What PedalCraft does

PedalCraft restarts the left puck every 50 seconds, so it keeps working. Now and then a press during the restart can be missed for about a second. Riding in the Zwift app once in a while also unlocks it for a longer time.

Other things to check

  • Update the Click firmware in the Zwift Companion app.
  • Quit Zwift and Zwift Companion on every device, so they don't grab the Click.
  • Press a button on each puck right before you connect, so it wakes up.
  • Replace the coin cell if the LED is weak.

FAQ

Is my Zwift Click v2 broken if the left button stops?

Probably not. The left puck is locked by firmware outside Zwift after about a minute.

Can I use the left button without Zwift?

Yes, PedalCraft restarts the left puck every 50 seconds to keep it working.

Does the right button have the same problem?

No, only the left puck is locked.
