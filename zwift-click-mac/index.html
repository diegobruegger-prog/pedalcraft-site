The Mac app

The PedalCraft Mac app runs natively on macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel. It keeps the Click connected in the background, holds your watts in ERG workouts from 20 minutes to 2 hours, runs an FTP ramp test, saves every ride and uploads it to Strava. A mini window shows your numbers on top of Netflix or YouTube, even in full screen. $39 once, all updates included.

FAQ

Does the Zwift Click work on a Mac without the Zwift app?

Yes. PedalCraft connects the Click over Bluetooth, in Chrome or in the native Mac app, without Zwift or an account.

Does it work in Safari?

No. Safari has no Web Bluetooth. Use Chrome or Edge, or the Mac app.

Is the Mac app a subscription?

No. It costs $39 once and includes all future updates.
